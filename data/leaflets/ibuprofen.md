# Ibuprofen tablets: patient information (SYNTHETIC)

## 1. What this medicine is and what it is used for
Ibuprofen is a non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID). It relieves pain, swelling, and fever, for example in muscle and joint pain, dental pain, period pain, and headache.

## 2. What to know before you take it
Do not take it if you have a stomach ulcer or bleeding, severe heart, kidney, or liver failure, or if aspirin or other NSAIDs have caused asthma or allergy. Tell your doctor if you have asthma, high blood pressure, heart disease, or are pregnant.

## 3. How to take this medicine
Always take this medicine exactly as your doctor or pharmacist has told you. Take it with or after food, with a full glass of water, to protect the stomach. Keep the gap between doses that your prescription states. Use it only for the number of days prescribed; long use increases the risk of stomach and heart problems.

If you forget a dose, take it when you remember unless the next dose is soon. Do not take a double dose.

## 4. Possible side effects
Common: indigestion, heartburn, feeling sick, stomach pain. Stop and seek help for black or bloody stools, vomiting blood, chest pain, severe headache, wheezing, or swelling.

## 5. Other medicines
Tell your doctor if you take aspirin, other painkillers, blood thinners, steroid tablets, blood pressure medicines, water tablets, or lithium.

## 6. How to store this medicine
Store below 30 degrees. Keep out of the sight and reach of children.
