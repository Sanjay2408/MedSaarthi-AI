# Metformin tablets: patient information (SYNTHETIC)

> Synthetic leaflet written for the MedSaarthi AI course project from general public knowledge. It is not an official manufacturer leaflet and must not be used for real medical decisions.

## 1. What this medicine is and what it is used for
Metformin lowers blood sugar in people with type 2 diabetes. It helps the body use its own insulin better and reduces the sugar made by the liver. It works best together with diet and exercise.

## 2. What to know before you take it
Tell your doctor if you have kidney or liver problems, heart failure, or drink a lot of alcohol. You may need to stop metformin for a short time before an X-ray scan with contrast dye or before an operation; your doctor will tell you when.

## 3. How to take this medicine
Always take this medicine exactly as your doctor has told you. Take the tablets with or just after meals to reduce stomach upset. Swallow them whole with water. Take the doses at the same times every day so you do not forget. This is usually a long-term medicine; keep taking it for as long as prescribed and return to your doctor before the prescription ends for a new prescription. Do not stop on your own even if your sugar readings look normal.

If you forget a dose, take the next dose at the usual time. Do not take a double dose.

## 4. Possible side effects
Very common at the start: feeling sick, diarrhoea, stomach pain, loss of appetite, metallic taste. These usually settle in a few weeks. Rare but serious: lactic acidosis, with vomiting, fast breathing, muscle cramps, and feeling very weak. Stop the tablets and get urgent help if this happens. Long-term use can lower vitamin B12.

## 5. Other medicines
Tell your doctor about water tablets, steroid tablets, and blood pressure medicines, which can change blood sugar.

## 6. How to store this medicine
Store below 30 degrees in a dry place. Keep out of the sight and reach of children.
