# Atorvastatin tablets: patient information (SYNTHETIC)

> Synthetic leaflet written for the MedSaarthi AI course project from general public knowledge. It is not an official manufacturer leaflet and must not be used for real medical decisions.

## 1. What this medicine is and what it is used for
Atorvastatin is a statin. It lowers cholesterol and other fats in the blood and reduces the risk of heart attack and stroke.

## 2. What to know before you take it
Tell your doctor if you have liver disease, muscle problems, an underactive thyroid, kidney problems, or drink a lot of alcohol. Do not take it if you are pregnant or breastfeeding.

## 3. How to take this medicine
Always take this medicine exactly as your doctor has told you. Swallow the tablet with water, with or without food, at the same time each day. Many prescriptions ask for it at night. Avoid large amounts of grapefruit juice. This is usually a long-term medicine: keep taking it and return to your health centre for a new prescription before the course ends.

If you forget a dose, take the next dose at the usual time. Do not take a double dose.

## 4. Possible side effects
Common: muscle aches, joint pain, headache, constipation, feeling sick. Tell your doctor straight away about unexplained muscle pain, tenderness, or weakness, especially with fever or dark urine.

## 5. Other medicines
Tell your doctor if you take some antibiotics (for example clarithromycin), antifungals, HIV medicines, ciclosporin, gemfibrozil, or colchicine.

## 6. How to store this medicine
Store below 30 degrees. Keep out of the sight and reach of children.
