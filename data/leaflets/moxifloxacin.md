# Moxifloxacin eye drops: patient information (SYNTHETIC)

> Synthetic leaflet written for the MedSaarthi AI course project from general public knowledge. It is not an official manufacturer leaflet and must not be used for real medical decisions.

## 1. What this medicine is and what it is used for
Moxifloxacin eye drops are an antibiotic used for bacterial infections of the eye, such as bacterial conjunctivitis (red, sticky eye).

## 2. What to know before you take it
Do not use them if you are allergic to moxifloxacin or other quinolone antibiotics. Do not wear contact lenses while you have an eye infection.

## 3. How to use this medicine
Always use this medicine exactly as your doctor has told you. Wash your hands. Tilt your head back and gently pull the lower eyelid down to make a small pocket. Hold the bottle upside down above the eye and squeeze gently to release one drop into the pocket. Close the eye and press lightly on the inner corner for about a minute. Do not let the dropper tip touch the eye or any surface. Use the drops in the eye or eyes stated on your prescription, for the full number of days, even if the eye looks better.

If you use another eye medicine, wait at least five minutes between them.

## 4. Possible side effects
Common: eye irritation, pain, or itching, and a bad taste in the mouth. Stop and seek help for swelling of the eyelids or face or for a widespread rash.

## 5. Other medicines
Tell your doctor about other eye drops or ointments you use.

## 6. How to store this medicine
Store below 25 degrees. Throw the bottle away 4 weeks after first opening. Keep out of the sight and reach of children.
