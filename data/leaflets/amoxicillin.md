# Amoxicillin capsules: patient information (SYNTHETIC)

> Synthetic leaflet written for the MedSaarthi AI course project from general public knowledge. It is not an official manufacturer leaflet and must not be used for real medical decisions.

## 1. What this medicine is and what it is used for
Amoxicillin is a penicillin-type antibiotic. It treats infections caused by bacteria, for example some chest, ear, throat, urinary, and dental infections. It does not work against viral infections such as the common cold or flu.

## 2. What to know before you take it
Do not take it if you have ever had an allergic reaction to penicillin or to any other antibiotic. Tell your doctor if you have kidney problems, glandular fever, or if you are pregnant or breastfeeding.

## 3. How to take this medicine
Always take this medicine exactly as your doctor has told you. Swallow the capsule whole with water. It can be taken before or after food. Space the doses evenly through the day, as prescribed. Keep taking the capsules for the full number of days on your prescription, even if you feel better earlier. Stopping too early can let the infection come back and can make bacteria resistant to antibiotics.

If you forget a dose, take it as soon as you remember, then continue at the usual times. Do not take two doses close together.

If you take too many capsules, contact your doctor or pharmacist; stomach upset is the most likely effect.

## 4. Possible side effects
Common: diarrhoea, feeling sick, skin rash. Stop taking it and get urgent help if you have swelling of the face or throat, difficulty breathing, or a widespread rash with blisters. Severe or bloody diarrhoea during or after treatment should be reported to a doctor.

## 5. Other medicines
Tell your doctor if you take allopurinol for gout, blood thinners, or methotrexate. Some antibiotics may reduce how well oral contraceptives work; ask your pharmacist for advice.

## 6. How to store this medicine
Store below 25 degrees in the original pack to protect from moisture. Keep out of the sight and reach of children. Return unused capsules to a pharmacy; do not keep them for future infections.
