# Pantoprazole gastro-resistant tablets: patient information (SYNTHETIC)

## 1. What this medicine is and what it is used for
Pantoprazole is a proton pump inhibitor. It reduces the amount of acid made in the stomach. It is used for acid reflux, heartburn, stomach and duodenal ulcers, and to protect the stomach when taking certain painkillers.

## 2. What to know before you take it
Tell your doctor if you have liver problems, low magnesium, low vitamin B12, or osteoporosis, or if you have unexplained weight loss, trouble swallowing, or vomit blood. These may need investigation before treatment.

## 3. How to take this medicine
Always take this medicine exactly as your doctor has told you. Take the tablet before a meal, usually before breakfast, so it works when the stomach starts making acid. Swallow the tablet whole with water. Do not chew or crush it, because the coating protects the medicine from stomach acid. Continue for the number of days on your prescription. Do not stop and restart on your own; ask your doctor if your symptoms return.

If you forget a dose, take it when you remember the same day, unless it is nearly time for the next dose.

## 4. Possible side effects
Common: headache, diarrhoea, constipation, wind, stomach pain. Long-term use may lower magnesium levels and may slightly increase the risk of bone fractures. Seek help for severe diarrhoea, skin reactions, or swelling.

## 5. Other medicines
Tell your doctor if you take HIV medicines, warfarin, methotrexate, or medicines for fungal infections, since stomach acid affects how some medicines are absorbed.

## 6. How to store this medicine
Store below 30 degrees in the original pack. Keep out of the sight and reach of children.
