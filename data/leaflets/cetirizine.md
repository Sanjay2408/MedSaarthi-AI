# Cetirizine tablets: patient information (SYNTHETIC)

## 1. What this medicine is and what it is used for
Cetirizine is an antihistamine. It relieves allergy symptoms such as sneezing, runny or itchy nose, watery eyes, and itchy skin rash (hives).

## 2. What to know before you take it
Tell your doctor if you have kidney problems, epilepsy, or difficulty passing urine. It may make some people sleepy; take care when driving or using machines, and avoid alcohol.

## 3. How to take this medicine
Always take this medicine exactly as your doctor or pharmacist has told you. Swallow the tablet with a glass of water, with or without food. If it makes you drowsy, taking it in the evening or at bedtime can help. Take it for the number of days on your prescription. If symptoms continue after the course, see your doctor again.

If you forget a dose, take the next one at the usual time. Do not take a double dose.

## 4. Possible side effects
Common: sleepiness, tiredness, dry mouth, headache, dizziness. Uncommon: stomach pain, restlessness. Seek help for allergic reactions such as swelling or breathing difficulty.

## 5. Other medicines
Tell your doctor if you take sleeping tablets, medicines for anxiety, or other antihistamines, since drowsiness can increase.

## 6. How to store this medicine
Store below 30 degrees. Keep out of the sight and reach of children.
