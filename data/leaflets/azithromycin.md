# Azithromycin tablets: patient information (SYNTHETIC)

## 1. What this medicine is and what it is used for
Azithromycin is a macrolide antibiotic. It treats some bacterial infections of the chest, throat, sinuses, ears, skin, and some sexually transmitted infections. It does not work against colds or flu.

## 2. What to know before you take it
Do not take it if you are allergic to azithromycin, erythromycin, or other macrolide antibiotics. Tell your doctor if you have liver or kidney problems, an irregular heartbeat, or myasthenia gravis.

## 3. How to take this medicine
Always take this medicine exactly as your doctor has told you. Take the tablet once a day at the same time, with water. Follow the food instruction on your prescription; some prescriptions say to take it one hour before food or two hours after food. Courses are often short; finish the full number of days even if you feel better.

If you forget a dose, take it as soon as possible, then continue as prescribed. Do not take two doses on the same day.

## 4. Possible side effects
Common: diarrhoea, feeling sick, stomach pain, headache. Seek urgent help for fast or irregular heartbeat, fainting, yellowing of the skin or eyes, severe skin reactions, or severe diarrhoea.

## 5. Other medicines
Tell your doctor if you take antacids, warfarin, digoxin, or medicines that affect heart rhythm. Antacids should not be taken at the same time as azithromycin.

## 6. How to store this medicine
Store below 30 degrees. Keep out of the sight and reach of children.
