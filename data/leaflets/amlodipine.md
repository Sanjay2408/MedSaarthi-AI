## 1. What this medicine is and what it is used for
Amlodipine is a calcium channel blocker. It relaxes blood vessels so blood flows more easily. It is used for high blood pressure and for some types of chest pain (angina).

## 2. What to know before you take it
Tell your doctor if you have liver problems, heart failure, very low blood pressure, or a recent heart attack. Tell your doctor if you are pregnant or breastfeeding.

## 3. How to take this medicine
Always take this medicine exactly as your doctor has told you. Take the tablet at the same time each day, with water, with or without food. Many people take it in the morning so it becomes part of a daily routine. Avoid large amounts of grapefruit juice. Blood pressure medicines are usually long-term: keep taking them even if you feel well, and see your doctor for a new prescription before the current one ends.

If you forget a dose, skip it if you remember only the next day. Never take two doses together.

## 4. Possible side effects
Common: ankle swelling, headache, flushing, tiredness, dizziness, palpitations. Stand up slowly if you feel dizzy. Seek help if you have chest pain that gets worse, fainting, or swelling of the face.

## 5. Other medicines
Tell your doctor if you take other blood pressure medicines, simvastatin, some antifungal or HIV medicines, or medicines for erectile dysfunction.

## 6. How to store this medicine
Store below 30 degrees, protected from light. Keep out of the sight and reach of children.
