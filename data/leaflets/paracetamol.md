# Paracetamol tablets: patient information (SYNTHETIC)

> Synthetic leaflet written for the MedSaarthi AI course project from general public knowledge. It is not an official manufacturer leaflet and must not be used for real medical decisions.

## 1. What this medicine is and what it is used for
Paracetamol is a pain reliever and fever reducer. It is used for headache, toothache, body ache, period pain, and fever, including fever after vaccination. It does not treat the cause of the pain or the infection behind a fever.

## 2. What to know before you take it
Tell your doctor or pharmacist if you have liver problems, drink alcohol regularly, are underweight, or take other medicines that may also contain paracetamol, such as many cold and flu remedies. Taking two products that both contain paracetamol is a common cause of accidental overdose.

## 3. How to take this medicine
Always take this medicine exactly as your doctor or pharmacist has told you. Swallow the tablet whole with a glass of water. It can be taken with or without food; taking it after food may be gentler on the stomach. Leave the gap between doses that your prescription states, and do not take more doses in a day than prescribed. If the medicine is prescribed only when needed, take it only when you have pain or fever and stop when you feel better. If the pain or fever lasts longer than the prescribed number of days, return to your doctor.

If you take more than you should, get medical help immediately, even if you feel well, because liver damage can appear later.

If you forget a dose, take it when you remember unless the next dose is due soon. Never take a double dose to make up for a missed one.

## 4. Possible side effects
Side effects are uncommon at prescribed doses. Rarely, skin rash, swelling, or breathing difficulty may occur; stop the medicine and seek help. Very rarely, blood disorders have been reported.

## 5. Other medicines
Tell your doctor if you take blood thinners such as warfarin, medicines for epilepsy, or medicines for tuberculosis, as these can change how paracetamol works.

## 6. How to store this medicine
Store below 30 degrees in a dry place, away from direct sunlight. Keep out of the sight and reach of children. Do not use after the expiry date on the strip.
